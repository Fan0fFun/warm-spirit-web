Privacy Policy for The Cocktail Memory Bar

Last Updated: December 19, 2025

1. Our Philosophy

The Cocktail Memory Bar ("we," "us," or "the App") was created to help you capture fleeting moments—the vibe, the flavor, and the feeling—without the need for perfect recall. We believe your memories belong to you alone. This policy outlines how we protect that privacy.

2. Data Collection & Usage

We operate on a strict "Local First" basis:

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • Local Processing: All memories you create are stored locally on your device (in your device's standard file system). We do not have access to your data, and it is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Photos & Camera: The App requests access to your Camera and Photo Library solely to allow you to capture or select images for your memory entries. These images are processed entirely on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

  • No Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) or advertising networks. We do not track your behavior inside the App.
  • Apple iCloud: If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your App data will be backed up to Apple's servers according to your personal iCloud settings. This is managed by Apple, not us.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

All financial transactions are handled directly by the Apple App Store. We do not process or store your credit card information. We may check your active subscription status to unlock premium features (like "Connoisseur Pass"), but this check is anonymous.

5. Data Retention

All data remains on your device. You have full control over it and can delete it at any time.
